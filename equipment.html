Safety Equipment

Here are essential safety items used in high-risk work environments.

Hard Hat

Protects the head from falling objects and collisions.

High-Visibility Vest

Ensures workers are visible in low-light and high-traffic areas.

Protective Gloves

Prevents cuts, burns, and chemical injuries to hands.

Safety Boots

Steel-toe boots designed to protect feet from heavy impacts.

Safety Goggles

Shields the eyes from dust, debris, and harmful chemicals.

Ear Protection

Used in loud environments to protect hearing over time.
